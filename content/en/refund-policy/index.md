We want paying for this site to feel low-risk. This page explains the two ways a refund can
happen: a money-back request on your first payment, and a per-contact refund when a specific
reveal turns out to be invalid.

## Money-back on your first payment

If you are not satisfied after your **first** paid payment on this site, you can request a
money-back within **7 days** of that payment from your account's billing page. This applies once,
to your first paid payment only — it is not a recurring or per-payment guarantee. Approved
requests are refunded to your original payment method through our payment processor; we aim to
process approved requests promptly, and you will be notified once it is complete.

## Refunds for an invalid contact

If you reveal a company's contact details and one of them turns out to be invalid, report it from
that company's page (choose "invalid contact" as the reason). We re-verify the contact:

- If we confirm it is genuinely invalid, we refund the credits you spent on that specific contact.
- If we confirm it is actually valid, or cannot confirm either way within a reasonable time, no
  automatic refund applies for that report, though the negative signal still helps us
  re-prioritise re-verification of that contact for other users.

This process exists specifically to make "I paid for a contact and it didn't work" right, without
requiring you to go through a separate support request.

## What is not covered

- Credits spent on a **Check a buyer** result, or on exporting rows from your buyer list, are not
  refundable individually — those actions deliver their result (a check outcome, an export file)
  regardless of what you later decide to do with it.
- A contact being valid but the buyer not being a good fit, not replying, or not placing an order
  is not grounds for a refund — see [what we promise and don't promise](/promise) for why we
  cannot guarantee outreach outcomes.
- Free monthly credits and free-tier usage are not payments and are not eligible for a money-back
  request.

## How to request

Money-back requests and invalid-contact reports are both made from within your account. If you
have a billing question this page does not answer, contact support from your account page and we
will help directly.
