We want you to know exactly what you are paying for, and just as importantly, what you are not.

## What we promise

- **A faster way to find companies that plausibly buy your product.** We combine shipment
  records, company registries, business directories, company websites, online marketplaces and
  trade-show lists to build a searchable list of overseas companies, organised by product and
  country.
- **Evidence, not just a name and address.** Wherever we can, we show you *why* a company appears
  for your product — a shipment record, a directory listing, a website mention — and where that
  evidence came from, so you can judge its strength yourself.
- **Checks that reduce risk before you commit time or money**, covering things like whether the
  company appears to be a registered entity, whether its website is consistent with the company
  name, roughly how long its domain has existed, whether its contact email looks like a company
  address, whether we see recent trade activity, and a sanctions/denied-party list screening.
  These checks make a bad outcome less likely; they cannot make it impossible.
- **Tools built for the realities of cold outreach**: an HS code helper, a first-export checklist,
  a scam red-flag guide, and drafting help for your outreach emails.
- **Contact details you pay for, delivered or refunded.** If a contact we reveal turns out to be
  invalid, our reveal, refund and reporting process (see the [refund policy](/refund-policy)) is
  built to make that right.
- **Clear, plain-language pricing.** Every credit-based action on this site is priced up front —
  see the [pricing page](/pricing) — with no hidden charges.

## What we do not promise

- **We do not promise you an order.** Nothing on this site can guarantee that any company you
  contact will reply, negotiate, or ever place an order with you. Finding and reaching the right
  company is only the first step in exporting; closing a deal depends on your product, your price,
  your relationship-building, and the buyer's own needs.
- **We do not certify or vouch for any company.** The checks we run reduce risk; they are
  automated signals from public and licensed sources, not a background investigation, a credit
  check, or a personal endorsement. A company passing our checks is not a claim that it is safe to
  trade with, only that it passed those specific, limited checks.
- **We do not promise complete coverage.** We hold more data for some countries and products than
  others — see the [coverage policy](/coverage) — and real buyers you have not found through us
  will exist for almost every product.
- **We are not a substitute for your own diligence.** Secure payment terms, sample verification,
  and your own judgement about a new counterparty remain your responsibility, especially for a
  buyer you have not traded with before. Read the [scam red-flags guide](/scam-red-flags) and use
  **Check a buyer** for any new contact.
- **We are not a customs broker, freight forwarder, bank, or law firm.** Nothing on this site is
  customs, tax, legal or financial advice; confirm anything specific to your shipment with the
  relevant professional.

## If something goes wrong

If a contact you paid to reveal turns out to be invalid, or a company clearly does not match what
we showed you, report it from that company's page. See the [refund policy](/refund-policy) for
how that is handled.
