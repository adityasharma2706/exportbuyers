Spices (HS Chapter 9 — pepper, cardamom, turmeric, chilli, cumin and others) are supported by the
**Spices Board India**, a statutory body under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
Registration with the Spices Board (an Exporter Certificate of Registration, in addition to your
IEC) is required specifically for spice exporters, and the Board also runs quality certification,
market development and buyer connect programmes.

Food-category exports like spices commonly need destination-country food safety compliance
(for example FSSAI-equivalent registrations abroad, pesticide-residue limits) alongside India's
own export policy — check these early, since they can affect your production process, not just
your paperwork.

*This page names the body commonly associated with this HS chapter as a starting point. Confirm
current requirements on the official Spices Board or DGFT website.*
