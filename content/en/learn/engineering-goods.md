Engineering goods — iron and steel products, machinery, electrical equipment, vehicles and parts,
and ships (broadly HS Chapters 72–73 and 82–89) — are India's largest single export category by
value, supported by the **Engineering Export Promotion Council (EEPC India)**. EEPC runs
buyer-seller meets, international delegations and market studies across sub-sectors from auto
components to capital goods.

Because "engineering goods" spans such a wide HS range, double-check your exact 8-digit ITC-HS
code and export policy before quoting — some categories (certain defence-adjacent or dual-use
items) carry licensing conditions that general machinery does not.

*This page names the council commonly associated with this HS range as a starting point. Confirm
current details on the official EEPC India or DGFT website.*
