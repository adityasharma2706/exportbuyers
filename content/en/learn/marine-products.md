Fish, shrimp, and other seafood and marine products (HS Chapter 3, and prepared/preserved
seafood under Chapter 16) are supported by **MPEDA (Marine Products Export Development
Authority)**. Registration with MPEDA (separate from your IEC) is required for marine product
exporters, and MPEDA also sets quality standards, runs processing-plant approvals, and supports
market development.

Marine exports have some of the strictest cold-chain, traceability and destination-country food
safety requirements of any export category (for example EU or US import health certification).
Confirm these requirements — and the processing-plant approvals they may require — well before
you commit to your first order.

*This page names the body commonly associated with this HS range as a starting point. Confirm
current requirements on the official MPEDA or DGFT website.*
