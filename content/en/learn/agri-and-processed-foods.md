Fresh and processed agricultural products — fruit and vegetables, cereals, processed foods and
non-alcoholic beverages (broadly HS Chapters 7–12 and 19–22, excluding spices and marine
products, which have their own dedicated bodies) — are supported by **APEDA (Agricultural and
Processed Food Products Export Development Authority)**. APEDA registration (separate from your
IEC) is required for many products in this category, and APEDA also runs quality infrastructure
support, market studies and buyer connect programmes.

Food-category exports commonly require destination-country food safety compliance (labelling,
pesticide-residue limits, phytosanitary certificates) in addition to India's own export policy —
these can affect your production and packaging process, so check them well before your first
shipment, not just before you ship.

*This page names the body commonly associated with this HS range as a starting point. Confirm
current requirements on the official APEDA or DGFT website.*
