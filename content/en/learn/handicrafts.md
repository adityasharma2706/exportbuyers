Handcrafted goods spanning several HS chapters (wood and cane products, hand-knotted carpets,
pottery and ceramics, glassware, metal handicrafts, and decorative items) are supported by
**EPCH (Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts)**. EPCH runs India's major handicrafts trade
fairs and buyer-seller meets and issues RCMC registration for this sector.

Because "handicrafts" spans so many different HS chapters, confirm your product's specific
8-digit ITC-HS code rather than relying on the general category — export policy, duty benefit
rates and packaging requirements can differ significantly between, say, a wooden item and a metal
one even within the same broad "handicrafts" description.

*This page names the council commonly associated with this HS range as a starting point. Confirm
current details on the official EPCH or DGFT website.*
