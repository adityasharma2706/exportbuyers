Leather, leather goods and footwear (HS Chapters 41–43 and 64) are supported by the **Council for
Leather Exports (CLE)**, headquartered in Chennai. CLE supports market development, participation
in international leather and footwear fairs, and RCMC registration that unlocks export benefit
schemes for this sector.

If your product uses genuine leather, note that some raw-hide and semi-finished leather items
carry export policy conditions — always check your product's 8-digit ITC-HS export policy (see
the [HS and ITC-HS guide](/learn/hs-and-itc-hs)) before you commit to an order.

*This page names the council commonly associated with this HS range as a starting point. Confirm
current details on the official CLE or DGFT website.*
