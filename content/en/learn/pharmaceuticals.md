Pharmaceutical and bulk-drug products (broadly HS Chapters 29 and 30) are supported by
**Pharmexcil (Pharmaceuticals Export Promotion Council of India)**, which runs international
delegations, buyer-seller meets and RCMC registration for this sector.

Pharmaceutical exports carry some of the strictest regulatory requirements of any export
category — manufacturing licences, destination-country drug regulator registration, and specific
labelling rules commonly apply in addition to India's own export policy. Treat the guidance on
this site as a starting point only, and involve a regulatory consultant before your first
international shipment.
