Precious and semi-precious stones, pearls, and gold, silver and diamond jewellery (HS Chapter 71)
are supported by the **Gem & Jewellery Export Promotion Council (GJEPC)**. GJEPC runs India's
major jewellery trade shows, buyer-seller meets, and RCMC registration for this sector.

This category carries some of the tightest compliance requirements of any export sector —
Kimberley Process certification for rough diamonds, hallmarking rules, and high-value shipment
security and insurance considerations all commonly apply. Build extra time for compliance and
logistics planning into your first export.

*This page names the council commonly associated with this HS range as a starting point. Confirm
current details on the official GJEPC or DGFT website.*
