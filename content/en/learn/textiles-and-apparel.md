Textile and apparel products (HS Chapters 50–63: yarns, fabrics, made-ups, knitted and woven
garments) are supported by several sector councils depending on your exact product:

- **AEPC (Apparel Export Promotion Council)** — for ready-made garments (broadly Chapters 61–62).
- **Texprocil (The Cotton Textiles Export Promotion Council)** — for cotton yarn and fabric.
- **SRTEPC, CEPC, PDEXCIL, HEPC** and others — cover synthetic and rayon textiles, carpets, powerloom
  products, and handlooms respectively.

Registering for the RCMC (Registration-cum-Membership Certificate) with the council matching your
exact product unlocks export benefit schemes and buyer-seller meet invitations. Check DGFT's
council directory or [Trade Connect](/learn/trade-connect-and-epcs) to confirm the right council
for your specific HS code, since the mapping has some overlap between councils.

*This page names the councils commonly associated with this HS range as a starting point. Confirm
the current, exact council mapping for your product on the official DGFT or council website.*
