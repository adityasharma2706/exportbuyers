Basic and speciality chemicals, dyes, cosmetics and allied products (broadly HS Chapters 28, 29,
32–34 and 38) are supported by **CHEMEXCIL (Basic Chemicals, Cosmetics and Dyes Export Promotion
Council)**. It offers market intelligence, participation support at international chemical trade
fairs, and RCMC registration.

Chemical exports are one of the categories most likely to carry regulatory conditions (hazardous
substance rules, destination-country registration such as REACH in the EU) in addition to
India's own export policy, so build extra time into your first shipment for compliance checks.

*This page names the council commonly associated with this HS range as a starting point. Confirm
current details on the official CHEMEXCIL or DGFT website.*
