Beyond this site, the Government of India runs several free resources for exporters that are
worth using alongside your search here.

## DGFT Trade Connect e-Platform

[Trade Connect](https://www.dgft.gov.in) is DGFT's platform bringing together market
intelligence, financing options, logistics support and mentorship for exporters, especially
first-time and MSME exporters. It is free to use and product-agnostic — every HS chapter is
covered, so it is a good starting point regardless of what you make.

## Export Promotion Councils (EPCs)

An EPC is a sector-specific body, usually set up with government backing, that promotes exports
of a particular category of product: market intelligence, buyer-seller meets, trade fair
participation support, and a **Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC)** that unlocks
several export benefit schemes. Most products map to one main EPC by HS chapter — this Learn
section has a short guide for several major categories (textiles, leather, engineering goods,
chemicals, gems & jewellery, spices, handicrafts, pharmaceuticals, agricultural and processed
foods, and marine products); open your product's guide from the list below, or search DGFT's
council directory if your product is not covered yet.

## Indian missions abroad

India's embassies, high commissions and consulates run commercial wings that help Indian
exporters connect with buyers, chambers of commerce and government contacts in that country. They
can be a useful, free channel for market entry support, especially in markets where you do not
yet have a distributor or agent relationship.

## How these fit with this site

This site helps you find and check specific buyer companies quickly. Trade Connect, your EPC and
your local Indian mission are complementary, slower-moving resources for market intelligence,
credibility (RCMC membership), and trade-fair or delegation opportunities. Using both together is
normal practice for active exporters.
