## HS code / ITC-HS code

HS (Harmonized System) is the international 6-digit code used to classify a product for customs
worldwide. India extends this to an 8-digit **ITC-HS** code, and the 8th digit is what decides
your product's Indian export policy — free, restricted, prohibited, or reserved for a state
trading enterprise. Read the full [HS and ITC-HS guide](/learn/hs-and-itc-hs).

## IEC (Importer-Exporter Code)

A 10-digit code issued by DGFT that every business needs before it can legally import or export
from India. It is a one-time registration, linked to your business PAN. Read the full
[IEC guide](/learn/iec).

## Incoterms

A standard set of trade terms, published by the International Chamber of Commerce, that fix
exactly where the seller's responsibility for cost, risk and delivery ends and the buyer's
begins. Read the full [Incoterms guide](/learn/incoterms).

## FOB (Free On Board)

An Incoterm meaning you pay for and arrange everything up to loading the goods onto the ship at
the Indian port. The buyer takes over cost and risk, including sea freight and insurance, from
that point.

## CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight)

An Incoterm meaning you pay for the goods, the sea freight and the marine insurance all the way
to the buyer's destination port. Risk still passes to the buyer once the goods are loaded on the
ship.

## MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)

The smallest quantity of a product you are willing to sell in a single order. Read the full
[MOQ guide](/learn/moq).
