This site runs on a simple credit system: you pay only for the specific actions below, and every
price is shown to you before you spend anything.

## What each action costs

- **Revealing a buyer's contact details:** {{price:reveal}}.
- **Revealing several buyers' contact details at once:** {{price:reveal_bulk_each}} per company
  (for example, revealing 5 companies at once costs {{price:reveal_bulk_each:5}} in total).
- **Running Check a buyer** on a company you paste in yourself: {{price:check_buyer}}.
- **Exporting a row of your buyer list** to a file: {{price:export_row}}.

Prices for some countries may carry a small surcharge multiplier, shown to you at the moment you
take the action, before you confirm it. If we cannot load the current price for any reason, the
action is paused rather than charged — we never guess a price.

## Free usage

Every account, including free accounts, gets a small number of credits granted automatically each
month at no cost, plus a limited number of free monthly uses for common actions like revealing a
contact or running a check. Your current balance and this month's free allowance remaining are
always visible on your account page before you spend anything.

## What is never charged for

Searching for buyers, browsing market pages, using the HS code helper, saving companies to your
shortlist, and reading the Learn area are all free, with no credit cost and no limit tied to your
plan.

## Paying for credits

If you need more than your monthly free allowance, you can top up your credit balance from your
account page. Every credit purchase, and every credit spent, is recorded in your account's credit
history so you can see exactly what you paid for.

## If you are not satisfied

See the [refund policy](/refund-policy) for your first-payment money-back window, and for how we
handle a specific contact that turns out to be invalid.
