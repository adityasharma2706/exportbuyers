"Coverage" is our word for how much buyer data we hold for a given country and product. We show
it as a label — **Strong**, **Partial** or **Limited** — next to search results and on market
pages, so you know how much confidence to place in the list you are looking at, before you spend
time or credits on it.

## How the label is decided

- **Strong** coverage means we hold shipment-record-backed data for a meaningful number of
  companies in that country and product, recently checked.
- **Partial** coverage means we have some data — often from business directories and company
  websites rather than shipment records, or from a smaller number of recently-checked companies —
  enough to be useful, but not yet enough for a Strong label.
- **Limited** coverage means we have few sources for that country and product so far. When we
  have very little data for a specific product, we sometimes show a country-level label instead,
  based on all products for that country, and say so explicitly.

## What the label does not mean

A Strong or Partial label describes how much data *we* hold, not how many real buyers exist in
that market. A country or product with a Limited label may still have a large, active import
market — we simply have less visibility into it yet. Conversely, a Strong label does not mean we
have found every buyer; new companies enter and leave a market continuously, and our sources are
never perfectly complete.

## How coverage changes over time

We add sources and refresh data on an ongoing basis, so a Limited label today does not mean
Limited forever. If a country or product matters to you and current coverage is thin, saving a
search lets us notify you as new matches appear.
